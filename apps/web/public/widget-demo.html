How To Embed On Any Client's Website

Copy this single <script> tag before the closing </body> tag of any website: 

<script
  src="http://localhost:3002/widget.js"
  data-bot-name="Nexus Support AI"
  data-company="Nexus Solutions"
  data-primary-color="#0284c7"
  data-quick-questions="What services do you offer?;How do I contact sales or support?;What are your hours?"
  data-web-url="http://localhost:3002"
  defer>
</script>
